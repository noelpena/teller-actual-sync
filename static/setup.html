Finding Your Sync ID

  1. Open Actual Budget
  2. Go to Settings (gear icon)
  3. Click "Show Advanced Settings"
  4. Copy the Sync ID

Mapping Bank Accounts

After saving, head to the admin dashboard's Account Mappings tab to pair each connected bank account with an Actual Budget account.
